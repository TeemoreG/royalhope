Did You Know?

67% of premature deaths are linked to conditions that could have been detected early through regular health screenings.

Every year, over 500,000 Kenyans skip or delay necessary health consultations due to lack of awareness, cost concerns, or simply not knowing where to start. This leads to advanced disease progression, higher treatment costs, and in many cases — preventable complications.

Early detection saves lives. Don't become a statistic. A simple consultation today could prevent a serious health crisis tomorrow.
